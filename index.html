Why games are different

Economies

An AI character that can hand out gold or items can be talked into handing them out.

Minor safety

T- and E-rated games put AI features in front of young players.

In-world social engineering

Players lie to characters in character, and the AI has only their word to go on.

Field note

This is already happening in shipped games. Players of Where Winds Meet tricked its AI NPCs into handing out quest rewards they had not earned. Source: GameSpot
