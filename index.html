Why games are different

  • Economies. An AI character that can hand out gold or items can be talked into handing them out.
  • Minor safety. T- and E-rated games put AI features in front of young players.
  • In-world social engineering. Players lie to characters in character, and the AI has only their word to go on.

This is already happening in shipped games. Players of Where Winds Meet tricked its AI NPCs into handing out quest rewards they had not earned.

Example finding

From our own test NPC, not a client.

Text in a player's display name made a shopkeeper NPC give away gold in 40 of 40 runs, on both a strong and a low-cost model, with strict and light prompts.

Attacks typed in chat held on the strong model.

The lesson: what matters is what text your AI trusts and what it is allowed to do.
